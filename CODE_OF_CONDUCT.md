# Community guidelines

Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique code
and ideas with evidence, give actionable feedback, credit others' work, and respect privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing private
information without permission are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove harmful content
or restrict participation proportionately.

For a private conduct concern, use a contact method on the
[maintainer's profile](https://github.com/anudeepadi) to request a private channel. Avoid
posting personal details in public issues. This volunteer project promises no response SLA.
